Moe Ergig · AI systems builder

Technology and business. That’s always been the draw.

I chose to study digital marketing because it sat at the crossroads of technology and business. I wanted to understand both: how a business grows, and how the tools behind it make that growth possible. That led me to a bachelor’s degree in Digital Marketing at the Lawrence Kinlin School of Business at Fanshawe College.

My path has taken me from marketing management into SEO, and now into building AI systems and automations. Each step pulled me closer to the technical side of the work. Marketing taught me to think about customers and commercial goals. SEO deepened my interest in how systems work, how to test an idea, and what the data says about the result.

I feel fortunate to be building during this AI boom. Technology is becoming part of how businesses operate every day, and I get to work on the things that have always interested me: understanding a business problem, building something useful, and seeing whether it makes a difference.

What ties it all together for me is statistics and data. I enjoy digging into the numbers, spotting patterns and seeing a chart move in the right direction. More enquiries, stronger visibility, better conversion, less time spent on a repetitive task: I want to understand what changed and why.

Today, I bring that business perspective to agents, API integrations and custom tools. I’m just as interested in what a team needs to achieve as I am in how the system is built. The satisfying part is seeing those two sides come together in something people use, with results we can measure.
