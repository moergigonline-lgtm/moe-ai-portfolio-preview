Moe Ergig · AI systems builder

Marketing got me curious. Building kept me interested.

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Digital Marketing from the Lawrence Kinlin School of Business at Fanshawe College. I started out in SEO and AEO, thinking about how people find a business, what they need to know and what makes them take the next step.

Over time, I became just as interested in what happens behind the scenes: the follow-ups, repeated data entry, disconnected tools and small tasks that fill a working day. That curiosity led me into building AI agents, integrations and internal tools. I enjoy taking a business problem, understanding the moving parts and turning it into something useful.

My starting point is always the process. Automation needs a working workflow or a clear SOP to build on. I want to understand what the team does, why it works and where time is being lost before deciding what to automate. The aim is straightforward: streamline repetitive work and give people more time for the parts that need their judgement.

I’m interested in freelance, contract and full-time opportunities where I can combine that marketing perspective with practical building—working closely with the people who will use the system.
