How I work

Practical by design.

Automation is useful only if it survives contact with the actual business process.

01

Map the process

Identify the repetitive tasks, constraints, data sources and people involved.

02

Define the actions

Separate safe reads from writes, model permissions and decide when review is needed.

03

Build & integrate

Connect systems through APIs, design interfaces and put the automation in context.

04

Test the edge cases

Handle missing data, duplicate events, failures, rate limits and handover.
